The Course

Learn It.
Then Race It.

Join us for a morning of instruction, practice, and racing. Learn key skills in braking, maximizing the track, and race craft.

Our coaches will provide instruction and training, and help you as you practice those skills on the sim. The training will end with a competitive race between participants. While tailored to beginners, this course is a great opportunity for all skill levels.

$60 per participant · approx. 2.5 hours

What You'll Cover

The Skills

Braking

Where to brake, how hard, and how to release the pedal so the car actually turns in instead of pushing wide.

Maximizing The Track

Racing line, apexes and track limits — using every inch of the road to find time you didn't know was there.

Race Craft

Starts, defending, overtaking cleanly, and keeping your head when there's a car alongside you.

Do I need any experience?

No. The course is built for beginners — if you've never used a sim wheel before, this is the right starting point.

I already race. Is it worth it?

Yes. While tailored to beginners, it's a great opportunity for all skill levels — most drivers find time in braking and track usage.

What should I bring?

Nothing. We supply the rigs, the gear and the coaching. Comfortable shoes help.

Can I book for a group?

Yes — get in touch and we'll arrange a private Academy session for your group.
